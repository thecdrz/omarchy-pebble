# Pebble beta test

Use Pebble normally for at least three days. Do not deliberately trigger every
feature on the first day; the test is whether its life unfolds naturally.

## Daily questions

1. Did Pebble feel alive without demanding attention?
2. Which behavior delighted you, and which became repetitive?
3. Did Pebble ever face the wrong way, change apparent size, jump position, or
   collide awkwardly with bar widgets?
4. Was the journal understandable at a glance?
5. Did Quiet, Normal, Lively, Snooze, and Reduced Motion behave as expected?
6. Did Pebble interfere with clicking, fullscreen applications, or work?
7. Would you leave it enabled permanently? Why or why not?

## Test matrix

Record Omarchy version, display count/scales, bar position/height, theme, test
duration, and whether the machine slept or changed monitors during the test.

## Evidence to return

- a short screen recording of any visual defect;
- the relevant time and action;
- `manifest.json` version;
- optional state summary (remove anything you do not wish to share);
- a 1–5 score for delight, calmness, clarity, animation polish, and reliability.

State files should never be requested as a condition of support. They contain no
content from other applications, but they still belong to the user.
