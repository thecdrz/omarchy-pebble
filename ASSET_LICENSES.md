# Asset provenance

All Pebble penguin animation frames, habitat art, and internal companion concept
images in `assets/` were created specifically for this project and are distributed
under the repository's MIT license.

Some project artwork was developed with generative image tooling and then cropped,
normalized, sequenced, and validated for use in the Omarchy bar. No third-party
game sprites, character brands, fonts, sound recordings, or icon packs are bundled.
